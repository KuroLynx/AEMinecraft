## AEMinecraft v0.7.0-rc.4

**Pre-release.** Fourth test build of v0.7.0. Stay on v0.6.2 if you want a stable game.

This RC only lists what changed since the **[v0.7.0-rc.3 release notes](https://github.com/KuroLynx/AEMinecraft/releases/tag/v0.7.0-rc.3)**. For everything new in v0.7.0, see the **[v0.7.0-rc.1 release notes](https://github.com/KuroLynx/AEMinecraft/releases/tag/v0.7.0-rc.1)**.

### Changed

- **The Biome Finder screen is a map.** Right-clicking the Biome Finder now shows a top-down map of the biomes around you, next to a searchable list of every biome in your dimension. Click a spot on the map or a name in the list to track it.
- **An arrow points the way.** While you track a biome, a small arrowhead floats just ahead of your view and turns toward it, like a compass needle. It faces you while you glide, swim or crawl. Its textures live in `textures/misc/biome_finder_arrow/`, so resource packs can replace them.
- **Clearing the target.** The biome you're tracking sits at the top of the screen with a ✕ to stop tracking it. Tracking also stops on its own once you walk into that biome.

### Upgrading from rc.3

- **Only the mod jar changed.** Keep the rc.3 apworld and your YAMLs.
