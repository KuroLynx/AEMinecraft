## AEMinecraft v0.7.0-rc.5

**Pre-release.** Fifth test build of v0.7.0. Stay on v0.6.2 if you want a stable game. rc.6 will be the last release candidate before v0.7.0, with a few more quality-of-life features.

This RC only lists what changed since the **[v0.7.0-rc.4 release notes](https://github.com/KuroLynx/AEMinecraft/releases/tag/v0.7.0-rc.4)**. For everything new in v0.7.0, see the **[v0.7.0-rc.1 release notes](https://github.com/KuroLynx/AEMinecraft/releases/tag/v0.7.0-rc.1)**.

Most of this RC comes from player reports. Thanks to everyone who sent them; every report was checked one by one.

### Hotfix (apworld re-uploaded 2026-10-23)

- **Generation could run out of memory and crash the PC.** A BACAP slot with glitch logic and every lock on used more than 11 GB while building its logic, and nothing was freed between Minecraft slots, so a multiworld with several of them ran the machine out of memory. The same slot now peaks around 3 GB, and each slot's memory is released before the next one starts. The logic and placements are unchanged: the same seed gives the same game as before. **If you downloaded the rc.5 apworld before this note, download it again.** The mod jars did not change.

### New options

- **`split_structure_variants`** (default `false`). Structures that only differ by biome now share one unlock: one `Structure Unlock: Village` opens all five villages, and the same goes for Ruined Portal (7), Ocean Ruin (2), Shipwreck (with beached), Mineshaft (with mesa) and, on 26.3, Abandoned Camp (18). The groups come from the game's own structure tags, so a datapack structure that joins one of those tags joins the group. The Structure Unlocks tab shows one tile per group and lays its tiles out as a full grid. Set it to `true` to get one item per variant, as before.
- **`combat_logic`** (default `true`). A hard fight is only in logic once you have Sword or Axe Handling, Material Handling up to iron, a reliable food supply and, when `inventory_lock` locks armor slots, the first two of them. Hard fights are raids, the Trial Chambers' ominous challenge (Trial Omen, ominous vault) and bosses. Everything that goes through one inherits it, such as raid gifts.
- **`exploration_logic`** (default `true`). An advancement that only asks you to go somewhere (Adventuring Time, Atlantis?, Subspace Bubble…) is only in logic once you have a reliable food supply (Hoe Handling, or one of Cow, Pig, Chicken, Sheep, Rabbit, Mooshroom, Cod or Salmon) and, with `inventory_lock`, a slot to carry it in.
- Both logic options only change where items are placed. The tracker still shows such a check yellow when you could already try it. Set them to `false` for the old behaviour.

### Logic fixes

Status effects and potions
- Status effects come from their real sources (mobs, food, beacons, stews, structures…) instead of "own a brewing stand". Darkness also comes from a Deep Dark's shriekers.
- Splash and lingering potions are brewed properly (gunpowder, dragon's breath), so Noxious Fumes and Gas! need the End. A 14% Trial Chambers dispenser no longer counts as a reliable source.
- A tipped arrow needs its lingering potion.
- Suspicious stew effects come from every flower that gives them (I'm gonna be sick!).
- Beacon effects need a beacon base.

Mobs and raids
- Raid advancements follow the mod's raid gate, and only pillagers and raids make raid captains.
- A banner with more layers than a loom can make is a raid captain's ominous banner (Superhero of the Village).
- Raid gifts are an unreliable source with unknown odds.
- Bees: honey and honeycomb need bees, a calm harvest needs a campfire, and honey bottles come from a full hive.
- The Happy Ghast needs a dried ghast, so the Nether (Lead Zeppelin).
- Trick or Treat! needs a mob that can actually wear a pumpkin.
- The Camel Husk comes with its Husk; wolf armor needs a tamed wolf (Armadoggo, Good as New, Shear Brilliance).
- Cured zombie villagers and igloo villagers count as villagers for every trade, even without a village.
- Villager trades are an unreliable item source when villages are not locked behind an unlock.
- Mob drops and structure loot of an unlock are reliable whatever the unlock's classification (leather, string: Help! I've been framed!, A Masterpiece!).
- Elder Guardian and Piglin Brute drops run out; wet sponges dry in the Nether (Sponge Miner).
- The ominous bottle: Trial Omen, Wind Burst and every item only the ominous vault or spawners hold need the Trial Chambers, a trial spawner mob and combat readiness.

Items, blocks and structures
- Mining a tiered block needs a pickaxe of that tier.
- A named enchantment's book comes from a librarian; random books are glitch only (Thin Ice!). An enchantment on an unnamed item asks for an item it can go on (Mace Windu), and the enchanting table only offers an enchantment on its primary items.
- Archaeology loot needs a brush.
- Wax scrapes off with an axe and crafted copper ages too (Lighten Up); aged copper blocks come from their plain block (Grate Expectations).
- Stripped logs need an axe or a village/camp palette (Shelfception).
- Dead coral needs living coral, and coral its Warm Ocean (Just a sample).
- Rare structure palette blocks are glitch routes, e.g. the copper golem statue in 2-14% of camps (Such a poser).
- An Overworld fossil's bone blocks are a glitch route (Boned, Paleontologist…).
- Naturally generated blocks that also have a recipe read as natural, with the Biome Finder when rare.
- Item colours ask for that dye (Colorful Cavalry).
- On 26.3, structure lists and structure tags in advancement conditions are read properly (Raidin' Master, Atlantis?, Quicksand, Ahoy!).
- New glitch routes: a Nether portal cast from water and lava, and string from a cobweb with a water bucket.
- Each armor slot unlocks one at a time in logic, in the mod's order.
- Fixed the replacement item used by item links, plando and start_inventory_from_pool.
- Glitch logic is much smaller on BACAP (export 5.5 MB → 1.7 MB), so trackers and tools handle it faster.

### Mod fixes

- **Raids:** when a raid can't start, the game tells you which raid mobs are still locked. The ominous bottle can't be drunk within 32 blocks of an unlocked village or Trial Chambers whose raid or trial can't start yet, and it says why.
- **Structures:** unlocked structures are rebuilt only into loaded chunks, without lag spikes or log spam (the mineshaft unlock lagged servers). Structures and mobs owed while a server was off are placed on the next start.
- **Abandoned Camps** show on the Structure Finder bar with a campfire and a biome badge.
- **Inventory lock:** inventory rows open from the hotbar up. Inventory Shuffle only shuffles unlocked slots. Dispensers can't put armor into a locked armor slot.
- **keep_inventory** keeps its share of XP, and a totem of undying no longer loses the kept items.
- **DeathLink:** `/aem deathlink mode random|all` picks whether a death kills one player or everyone on a server; saved in the world.
- **Tracker:** checks under a hidden advancement re-parent to the nearest visible one, and a tab root with checks below it shows (gray). Goal tiles go gray on their own count and blue once collected.
- The Biome Finder button follows the recipe book panel.
- The BACAP rewards marker now lives in the world's `data/` folder.
- `/aem dump` logs a failed pack dump and keeps it on screen, and the dump button writes every file.

### Upgrading from rc.4

- **Replace both the apworld and the mod jar.** Your YAMLs keep working; the three new options take their defaults. With the defaults, grouped structure unlocks shrink the item pool and the new logic options place combat and exploration progression a little later, so expect different seeds from the same YAML.
